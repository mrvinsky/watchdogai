The Problem

relies on a piece of plastic.

✕

The Hidden Risk

If a dealer cheats,
it's not just the player who loses.

Live casino providers are regulated by some of the strictest gaming authorities in the world — MGA, UKGC, Gibraltar. A single verified dealer fraud incident can trigger consequences that dwarf any short-term gain from operational savings.

🃏

Card Mucking

A dealer secretly swaps or palms cards to give accomplice players an advantage. Almost impossible to catch without frame-by-frame verification of who is at the table and when.

📡

Collusion Signaling

A dealer signals hole card values to a third party watching the live stream — using hand positions, chip placements, or timing patterns. Detection requires knowing exactly which dealer is on shift.

🔄

Unauthorized Substitution

A trained accomplice sits in a dealer's seat mid-session — after the rotation scan has already passed. The badge system has no way to catch this. A face system catches it in 100ms.

🎰

False Shuffle / Deal

A dealer uses sleight-of-hand to control card order in favour of a specific player. Only consistent identity tracking across sessions can expose patterns that reveal the fraud.

What regulators can do when fraud is proven at a licensed provider

⚖️

Regulatory Fines

MGA fines range from €23,000 to €375,000 per violation. Repeat incidents or systemic failures carry multipliers. UKGC fines have reached tens of millions of GBP for operators who failed to prevent fraud.

🚫

License Suspension or Revocation

A suspended license means zero live operations until investigation is complete. For a 45-table live studio running 24/7, even a 48-hour suspension represents a six-figure revenue loss.

📋

Operator Contract Terminations

Platform operators (Bet365, William Hill, PokerStars) hold providers fully liable for game integrity failures. A single fraud incident can trigger contract termination clauses worth millions in annual B2B revenue.

How It Works

Three steps. Zero friction.

The system runs across three simple screens — built for the people who actually use them, not for engineers.

1

HR Onboarding

HR uploads a photo, employee ID, and game nickname. InsightFace extracts a 512-D vector stored permanently in Redis. Done once — works forever.

2

WFM Shift Plan

Supervisor sets table shift plan via rapid-scan barcode or panel. System maps scheduled dealer IDs to live Sony RTSP camera streams.

3

Zero-Friction Dealer

Camera recognizes dealer in 100ms. Corner badge displays game nickname ("✓ Ace"). Screen shows clean real-time clock — zero popups, zero stress.

4

30-Min Shift & Audit

AI monitors all 45 tables 24/7. Auto-detects intruders, multi-face violations, and triggers alerts if a shift exceeds 30 minutes without rotation.
